# Sequoia Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

Sequoia is a vocabulary app: one word a day, and each word grows a tree. It is built so that what you do in it stays with you: on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

## What Sequoia collects

**Nothing.** Sequoia has no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking or third-party code, and it never sends data to us. Your data goes only to your own devices, your iCloud account and, if you use it, Game Center.

## Where your data lives

- **Your progress.** The words you've learned, your streak, your trees and your settings are stored on your iPhone. Sequoia's widgets read them there.
- **iCloud backup.** If you're signed in to iCloud, Sequoia keeps a copy of that progress in your iCloud account, so a new iPhone or a reinstall picks up where you left off. It lives in your private iCloud storage, which Apple runs under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and only you can access it. We can't see it.
- **Apple Watch.** If you use the watch app, your iPhone sends today's word and your tree to your paired Apple Watch directly, using Apple's WatchConnectivity.
- **Reminders.** Daily reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Sequoia doesn't use push notifications or a server.
- **Photos.** When you share a word card or a picture of your forest, you can save it to your photo library. Sequoia can add images there but can't see your photos.

Deleting Sequoia deletes the data on your devices. **Reset progress** in Sequoia's settings also erases the iCloud copy.

## Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Sequoia sends your streak length and tree count to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, so you and your Game Center friends can see them. Apple runs Game Center under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can turn Game Center off in **Settings → Game Center**, and Sequoia works the same without it.

## Children

Sequoia doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.
