# Word of the Day - App Clone Proposal

## 1. App Choice

### Word of the Day: Vocabulary

[View on IOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/word-of-the-day-vocabulary/id987136347)

### Why This App?

- The problem it solves is subjectively interesting to me
- The right balance between simple and engaging/challenging
- Compatibility with a small userbase: While a larger userbase could enable engaging social features for example, the app is still functional without one (unlike Reddit for example)
- The core user experience is more reliant on core app functionality / UI than external content (unlike Netflix for example)
- The app experience can especially benefit from features like widgets, notifications and watch support, which I would like to try implementing

## 2. The Fundamental Problem

### Users want a fast and unobtrusive yet habit forming method to improve personal vocabulary and diction

This core appeal is shown in the advertising screenshots in the app store with phrases like “Small Wins Every Day” and “Daily Challenges for Lasting Fluency”

Some users might be motivated by intellectual curiosity, others by career success or stronger communication.

## 3. What To Keep

#### “Word of the Day” daily check-in

This core system should accomplish the stated goal of an inobtrusive way to improve vocabulary effectively

#### Streaks and social incentives

Rewarding users with a points system incentivizes daily engagement and allows them to show off progress to friends. This lets users track their progress and encourages word-of-mouth.

#### An Effective Library of Words

Querying random words from Wiktionary would be a good first pass iteration, but not an optimal one.

Users need words that strike a delicate balance between being novel and specific, while also being useful and meaningful

## 4. What To Change or Remove

#### Overall simplified UI and core loop

The app should present the daily check in card on app open as the core feature. The existing UI and “today” section are cluttered and often redundant.

#### Obtrusive User Survey

When first engaging with the app users are locked behind a quite lengthy survey about their needs. A few questions like preference for level of time commitment are useful, but a lengthy amount of survey material goes against the core appeal of being unobtrusive!

#### Games

The app includes about six core games locked behind a pro subscription, which feel tacked on and disconnected from vocab improvement. The clone might include one or two games more directly aligned with learning words.
